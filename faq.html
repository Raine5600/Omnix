Website & Services

Your website is custom built for your business — not a generic template. We design it around your brand, services, and target customers to make sure it converts visitors into leads. Every site we build is unique to the business it represents.

You own your domain and your phone number. Omnix hosts the site. If you leave, the site files are handed over.

After every job, the customer gets a review request. The same request goes to every customer. We do not gate it by star rating, and we do not send only happy customers to Google.

The custom website is written for the services and the area you cover. Ranking on Google is not something we promise as part of Never Miss a Job.
